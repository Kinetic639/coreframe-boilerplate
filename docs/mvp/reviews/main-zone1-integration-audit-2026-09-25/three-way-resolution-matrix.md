For every file in `conflict-files.md`. "Main change" and "Zone 1 change" are summarized from the actual diffs read for this audit (merge-base → `origin/main`, merge-base → `HEAD`), not guessed.

## The single governing finding

`origin/main`'s own independent "dataview refactor phase 1" (`bde5f453`) **already fixed the exact branch-agnostic-cache-key bug Zone 1 Phases 2-3 fixed** — for all 4 of the same consumers (Locations, Inventory Balances, Inventory Movements, Inventory Products) — using a different, more general, newer mechanism: a `scope: DataViewScope` prop (a generic key-value record) plus a centralized `dataViewKeys`/`invalidateDataViewEntity` module, with a ready-made `dataViewScope.branch(organizationId, branchId)` factory. Confirmed by reading `origin/main`'s own versions of all 4 consumers: each already calls `scope={dataViewScope.branch(organizationId, branchId)}`. This single fact governs the resolution of 6 of the 8 real conflicts, plus 3 of the "clean but stale" auto-merges — it is not 9 independent judgment calls, it is one architectural decision applied consistently.
