## Ownership uniqueness check

Every requirement above appears in exactly one "Phase owner" cell. No requirement is listed twice with two different owners. The "ALREADY CORRECT" and "OUT OF SCOPE" rows are deliberately non-phase classifications (per the task's own Section 19 instruction that every item needs "a clear owner phase or explicit deferred status") — each such item is a single foundation the plan explicitly commits not to rework, cited once in the plan's "Verified-current foundation summary" and cross-referenced, not duplicated, wherever a later phase's dependency section might otherwise be tempted to re-verify it (e.g. Phase 5's re-sweep explicitly re-CONFIRMS rather than RE-IMPLEMENTS the permission-context bridge).
